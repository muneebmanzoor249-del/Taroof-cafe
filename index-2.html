Our Story

An everyday ritual, made a little more special.

Tucked into the Main Chowk of Tawheed Bagh, Taarof Cafe has become Sopore's go-to spot for a quiet coffee, a celebratory shake, or a plate shared with people you like. "Taarof" — the art of gracious hospitality — is the whole idea: warm service, clean spaces, and food made with care.

Guests keep coming back for the softy, the dry-fruit shake, and a team that treats every table like it matters.
